API Reference

🔑 One keyword per data set

CRU CL 2.0, WorldClim, CHELSA, TerraClimate, gridMET, NCEP, ERA5, AWAP, SILO and BARRA, swapped with weather_source and nothing else.

🗺️ Points or maps

The same model for one point, many points or every cell of a grid, returned as a RasterStack with named dimensions.

⛰️ Terrain from any DEM

Slope, aspect and horizon angles from Geomorphometry.jl, and weather corrected for elevation.

🧩 Built on Rasters.jl

Data from RasterDataSources.jl, held and analysed with Rasters.jl and DimensionalData.jl.

🌱 Soils and land cover

Soil profiles from SoilGrids and SLGA texture, albedo and roughness from land-cover classes.

🌳 Canopies

Microclimate.jl's multilayer canopy driven across a landscape.

🦎 From NicheMapR

The Julia counterpart of micro_global, micro_usa, micro_ncep and their relatives.

Manual ​

MicroclimateMapper.jl runs Microclimate.jl over points and grids, taking its weather, terrain, soil and surface inputs from spatial data sets. See the Introduction for the design, and For NicheMapR users for its origins in NicheMapR.

It is part of the BiophysicalEcology ecosystem for mechanistic niche modelling, and builds on RasterDataSources.jl, Rasters.jl, DimensionalData.jl and Geomorphometry.jl.
